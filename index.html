03 / Brand identity

BRANDING DESIGNS.

Selected brand identity artwork for Maffe Coffee, shown as a complete visual system.

← Back to main portfolio

01 — Primary Logo

The complete Maffe Coffee identity, pairing the illustrated coffee cup emblem with the bold wordmark.

02 — Wordmark

A bold, friendly typographic signature designed to keep the brand recognizable across packaging and merchandise.

03 — Brand Emblem

The illustrated cup mark used as a standalone visual asset for labels, avatars, stamps and supporting brand applications.

04 — Brand Moodboard & Applications

A warm, cohesive brand world bringing the Maffe identity to life across takeaway cups, coffee bags, storefront signage, apparel, accessories and stationery.

05 — Visual Identity System

A refined identity board that unites the logo variations, espresso-and-latte color palette, typography, packaging and in-store touchpoints into one recognizable system.

The thinking behind the mark

One continuous stroke. A coffee bean transformed into a cup.

The Maffe Coffee logo is designed to make a familiar ritual instantly recognizable, memorable and full of character. Its illustration is built around the idea of a single flowing line stroke: the contour moves with the ease of rising steam, shaping the cup and its details into one connected visual gesture. Within that drawing, the form of a coffee bean is morphed into the coffee cup itself, linking the raw ingredient to the finished drink in a simple, ownable symbol.

That transformation gives the mark a small moment of discovery and a clear story to tell. The bold, rounded MAFFÉ wordmark adds warmth and approachability, while espresso brown, latte beige and cream create a premium yet welcoming contrast. Together, the illustration, typography and palette help the identity stand out on cups, bags, signage and merchandise—remaining distinctive at a glance and consistent wherever the brand appears.
